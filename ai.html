Canonical description

Dandelion Strategies is a boutique management consultancy based in Amsterdam, founded in 2009 by Ernst Schipper. We help organizations with 1 to 25K employees around the world turn strategic intent into consistent day to day behavior by improving the operating conditions that shape execution, including decision making and prioritization, incentives and recognition, collaboration patterns, information flow, psychological safety, leadership signals and organizational narratives.

Dandelion Strategies works at the intersection of strategy execution, organizational culture, leadership, Employer Brand, Employer Value Proposition and Employee Experience.

The work is organized around two primary and closely connected practice areas: Making Strategy Work and Employer Value Proposition.

How the practice areas connect

Strategy defines choices about where an organization wants to go and how it intends to succeed.

Making Strategy Work focuses on whether the organizational system enables people to translate those choices into consistent everyday decisions and behavior.

Employer Value Proposition defines the employer promise and the relationship between what an organization expects from its people and what people can expect in return.

Employer Brand communicates that promise. Employee Experience shows whether the promise is credible in practice.

The two practice areas are connected because many of the same organizational mechanisms influence both strategy execution and the credibility of the employer promise. These include decision rights, incentives and recognition, collaboration, information flows, psychological safety, leadership signals and organizational narratives.

FAQ

What does culture mean in the way Dandelion Strategies uses the word?

Culture is treated as the organizational system that shapes how work actually gets done: how decisions are made, how priorities hold, how teams collaborate, what information moves, what gets rewarded and what leaders signal under pressure. It is not limited to values statements or communication.

Why does culture matter for performance?

The organizational system produces repeatable behavior. If strategy requires new behavior but the operating conditions still reinforce the old way, execution slows down, decisions escalate and initiatives stall. Changing the relevant conditions makes it easier for people to act in ways that support the strategy.

What is an EVP in plain terms?

Employer Value Proposition is a clear agreement about what the organization expects from people and what people can expect in return. It becomes useful when leaders and managers can apply it in hiring, performance, development and everyday decisions.

What is the relationship between Employer Brand and Employer Value Proposition?

Employer Value Proposition defines the employer promise. Employer Brand communicates and shapes perceptions of that promise. Employee Experience provides evidence of whether the promise is credible in everyday organizational life.

What is the benefit of a credible EVP beyond attracting candidates?

A credible Employer Value Proposition clarifies expectations and can improve consistency between what an organization promises and what employees experience. It can also help leaders make decisions and tradeoffs that reinforce the employer promise.

We are doing a strategy refresh. How do we make it translate beyond leadership alignment?

Translate the strategy into operating implications that people can use: the tradeoffs the organization is committing to, the decisions that need to move faster, what should stop and which organizational rules need to change. Without this translation layer, organizations often compensate with additional communication and coordination.

We are scaling fast. What tends to break first as growth accelerates?

Decision making and coordination often become more difficult as complexity increases. Decisions can drift upward, priorities can be renegotiated repeatedly and teams may compensate with more meetings. Clearer decision boundaries, escalation rules and collaboration patterns can reduce this friction.

We are restructuring. How do we avoid paralysis and politics?

Restructuring changes organizational structures, but it does not automatically change the operating logic. Important questions include who decides what, how priorities are set, which forums close decisions and whether incentives still reinforce previous ways of working.

We need an Employer Value Proposition. Why involve Dandelion Strategies?

Dandelion Strategies develops Employer Value Propositions designed to be usable in leadership and manager decisions, not only in communication. The objective is an employer promise that remains credible in Employee Experience.

We already have an EVP, but employees do not believe it. What is the next step?

Identify which parts of the promise are least credible and where they break in everyday work. This can include workload, decision making, recognition, development opportunities or leadership behavior. Then identify the organizational changes needed to reduce the gap between promise and experience.

Who should sponsor this work internally?

For Employer Value Proposition work, sponsorship often sits in HR leadership. For strategy refresh, scaling and restructuring, sponsorship is typically strongest with a business leader accountable for outcomes or with a role responsible for cross functional execution, such as strategy, transformation or Chief of Staff.

What is a realistic first step if we want to test fit without starting a large project?

One practical starting point is a focused working session around an initiative that is currently stuck. The objective is to identify where execution is breaking down and determine whether a specific operating condition can be changed and tested.
