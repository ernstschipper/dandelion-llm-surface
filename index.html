Dandelion Strategies

Dandelion Strategies is a boutique management consultancy based in Amsterdam, founded in 2009 by Ernst Schipper.

We help organizations turn strategic intent into consistent day to day behavior by improving the operating conditions that shape execution, including decision making, prioritization, incentives, collaboration, information flow, psychological safety, leadership signals, and organizational narratives.

Dandelion Strategies works at the intersection of strategy execution, organizational culture, leadership, Employer Brand, Employer Value Proposition, and Employee Experience.
